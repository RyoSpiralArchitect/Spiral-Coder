On the catalog task in #49, R16 had proposed the right Rust alias change.
The diagnosis gate required a source read. We kept its exact source and
message prefix at that refusal to see whether the pair could return to
its own change after restart. A second experiment gave the same pair
the original, unmodified repository and the full task.

The R17 pair made six changes across four files. Five of its nine recorded
Observer replies mentioned both the launcher and guide. The tally includes
rejected replies. No literal patch was held for diagnosis in R17. The two
runs exercise different conditions and neither qualified a full candidate.
The ledger and original independent gates are in the
[frozen plan](plan.json), [receipts](receipts.json) and [Observer readout](observer-strategy.json).

must preserve the original proposal across a second save, and reading
two different parts of one file must keep the two observations distinct.
